Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

What this is

shorts-factory is a private tool the owner of the enabledspirits channel uses to upload their own videos to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it uses

The tool asks YouTube and TikTok for permission to publish videos to the owner's accounts, and to read basic TikTok account details needed to post. The permission tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold or shared with anyone.

Visitors and viewers

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics, and the tool collects nothing about people who watch the videos. GitHub hosts this site and may keep its own technical logs.

YouTube and TikTok

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove a Google connection at any time from your Google account permissions. TikTok's handling of data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
